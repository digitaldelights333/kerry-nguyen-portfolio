### What is Kerry's PM process?
Kerry's process is a 4-phase loop. Starting with **Discover**, she finds the signal worth investigating.
In **Define**, she decides how far to go. Sometimes the right move is to improve one part of an existing workflow. Other times the right move is to re-imagine how the whole workflow happens, designing something new from first principles.
Then she **Develops** by prototyping, refining, and committing, and **Delivers** by launching, measuring outcomes, and iterating back to Discover.

### Cadence (product)
Cadence (Kiddom · 2026).  
The problem: Teachers had no single place to plan and pace instruction, relying instead on a patchwork of disconnected tools, paper guides, and habits.  
Impact: Made dedicated planning and pacing available to every teacher across every grade band and subject area, giving teachers a flexible way to adapt pacing to their students' needs and arrive prepared for instruction.

### Bilingual View & Print (product)
Bilingual View & Print (Kiddom · 2026).  
The problem: English learners needed Spanish-language support, but a full-translation toggle replaces English rather than supporting real bilingual learning.  
Impact: Bilingual expansion into Spanish-language and dual-immersion classrooms, enabling districts with growing demand for bilingual instruction to provide instructional materials in both English and Spanish.  
Link: View the full case study

### AI Tree Tool (product)
AI Tree Tool (Kiddom · 2025).  
The problem: As curriculum content scaled across 400+ course trees, manual review couldn't keep pace with mounting grammar, formatting, and broken-link errors — eroding user trust.  
Impact: Reduced content errors from 1,000+ to <100 after deploying the tool across the full 400+ course-tree library.  
Link: View the full case study

### Math Keyboard (product)
Math Keyboard (Kiddom · 2026).  
The problem: Students needed to enter real mathematical notation in written responses, but no single keyboard design works across every grade band from K–2 to high school.  
Impact: Enabled students across every grade band to express mathematical reasoning in authentic notation, while keeping the interaction model appropriately simple for younger learners and more powerful for advanced students.  
Link: View the full case study

### Digital Protractor (product)
Digital Protractor (Kiddom · 2026).  
The problem: Measuring angles on a digital assignment required a disconnected, multi-step workaround: draw on paper, measure with a physical protractor, then photograph and submit.  
Impact: Replaced a multi-step, paper-dependent workflow with a single in-tool interaction, allowing students to measure and submit their work digitally while eliminating photographed paper artifacts for teachers to interpret and grade.  
Link: View the full case study

### Student Calculator (product)
Student Calculator (Kiddom · 2026).  
The problem: Show Your Work questions needed calculator access for computation-heavy problems, but giving unrestricted access on every question risked undermining what was actually being assessed.  
Impact: Gave teachers control over calculator access at the question level, allowing them to match tools to what each question is designed to assess without restricting or enabling calculators across the entire assignment.  
Link: View the full case study

### State Adoption Course Builder (curriculum)
State Adoption Course Builder (Kiddom · 2025).

Impact: 45% faster course builds, while increasing confidence in standards alignment and reducing errors. More importantly, established a scalable workflow that can be reused across future state adoption builds, helping the team respond to new state opportunities faster and with greater confidence.

OpenSciEd & OpenStax State Adoptions (Kiddom · TX, FL, TN · 2023–25).  
The problem: OpenSciEd and OpenStax's NGSS-aligned curricula needed to align with each state's standards framework, requiring modifications and gap-filling content without diluting pedagogical integrity.  
Impact: Delivered adoption-ready curriculum across three states and multiple science disciplines, supporting districts with different standards requirements while preserving the integrity of the source curricula. The largest adoption serves 60,000+ students in Knox County, Tennessee.

Science Assessment Generator (Kiddom · 2024).

Impact: Turned a manual assessment-authoring workflow into a working generation tool that could produce alternative OpenSciEd assessments across formats and state testing contexts without replacing the pedagogical rigor of the source curriculum.
