Privacy

Kerry Nguyen Portfolio · Last updated October 12, 2026

This site includes an Availability section that shows when Kerry is open for a conversation. This page explains what that feature does with Google Calendar data.

What the app accesses

The site owner has authorized a small service, "Portfolio Availability," to read free/busy information from the owner's own Google Calendar. It uses the calendar.freebusy scope, which returns only the times a calendar is busy. It cannot read event titles, descriptions, attendees, locations, or any other event details.

How it is used

The service compares busy times against working hours and publishes only the resulting open time windows on the site. The busy times themselves are not stored or shown.

Visitors

Visitors do not sign in to anything and are never asked to connect a Google account. The availability feature collects no personal data from visitors. The site separately uses cookieless analytics (GoatCounter and PostHog) to count page views and clicks.

Sharing and storage

Google Calendar data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Open time windows are cached briefly (about 10 minutes) so the site loads quickly. Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account permissions page.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
